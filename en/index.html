I don't do boring marketing

Fifteen years building brand marketing at the intersection of strategy, creative, product, and business — so that meaning and emotion translate into recognition, loyalty, growth, and real business outcomes.

AliExpress, Yandex, TNT, Wildberries — these aren't just lines on my résumé. These are brands I worked with at the scale of major markets and millions of users.

Marketing as a system

I know how to build marketing as a system: from positioning and brand platform to the creative idea, production, team, campaign launch, and results measurement.

Worked in international markets — France, Germany, Brazil, China, Spain. Founded and sold a sports startup — I understand the real cost of errors, focus, and speed.

Meaning and emotion — my territory

Working with meaning and emotion is my core territory. That's what makes a brand not just visible, but needed — helping people understand, feel, and choose.

I studied neuromarketing and neuro-coaching at the Moscow Institute of Psychoanalysis — it sharpened how I read audience behaviour and understand why some brands stay in the mind.
