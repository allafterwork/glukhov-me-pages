Mechanics

Personal year-in-review — like Spotify Wrapped, but for a browser

Yandex Browser collected data about each user's behaviour in 2024 and packaged it into a personalised "I Did Great!" report. How many metres of web pages were scrolled, which AI features were used most, how many hours of video were translated — all of it became a personal story for each user.

The mechanics are simple: go to the page, press a button — and see your year. But behind that simplicity lies work with real user data that transforms faceless product statistics into a personal narrative. Everyone could feel like the main character of their own 2024.

Idea

Praise the user — not the product

"I Did Great!" — a title where the focus shifts from the browser to the person. Not "the browser did this for you," but "look how much you accomplished." This tone turns end-of-year statistics into a reward, and the advertising mechanic into a genuine compliment.

AI Inside

The year the browser got smarter

More than 10% of users actively used AI features in 2024. Text and video summarisation, real-time video translation, AI editor — all of this entered the report as part of each user's personal story. AI stopped being an abstract technology and became a familiar everyday tool.

Context

Data that builds loyalty

Personalised year-in-reviews are a proven format. Spotify Wrapped, Apple Replay, annual reports from streaming services reliably go viral every December. Yandex Browser did the same with its own material: data about real user behaviour became a tool not just for measurement, but for attachment to the product. When a browser knows you better than you know yourself — it's harder to leave.
