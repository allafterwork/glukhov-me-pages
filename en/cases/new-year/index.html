Concept

Small joys of a big year

The hardest part is finding a New Year insight that resonates with everyone. We needed to acknowledge the weight of the year — gently and with a positive spirit. Instead of pretending everything was fine, the campaign honestly said: the year was tough — but despite the stress and challenges, we never lost our optimism, humour, or love for ourselves and each other. That's exactly what AliExpress purchases helped with.

Ambassadors Maxim Galkin* and Anna Khilkevich show how small purchases turn into small joys.
