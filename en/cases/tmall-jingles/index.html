Concept

One brand — four languages

The task: differentiate Tmall from AliExpress — show that it's a platform for major international brands with fast delivery and competitive prices. The standard path: one image film. We went a different way: we recorded four jingles with real musicians in different genres and used targeting by musical preference.

A user who listens to indie-pop saw the Yana Blinder film. Folk fans saw FolkBeat. Rock audiences saw Rekord Orkest. Rap audiences saw VSIGMΣ. The ad stopped being an interruption — it matched what the person already wanted to hear.

Distribution

Genre-based targeting

Four versions ran as pre-rolls with targeting by musical interests. A person saw the genre they already listen to. This lowered the skip impulse and increased view-through rates.

Result

72M+ views

The campaign became one of the most-viewed in its category. Tmall branded searches tripled. The "jingles festival" mechanic was later repeated for the next campaign with Timati.
