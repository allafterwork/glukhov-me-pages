Global business trust

This was the first international campaign in AliExpress history fully developed and produced in Russia. Our colleagues in China trusted my team to create the communication for all of the brand's markets — we ran the tender, selected the agency, and launched a campaign that aired simultaneously in six countries.

Concept

Remind people about 11.11 — any way you can

The campaign insight: people invent increasingly absurd ways not to miss the year's biggest sale. A singer arranges musical notes into the numbers 11.11; an electrician cuts power to an entire district just to display the date on a scoreboard. A touch of grotesque, plenty of humour — and one unified signal for six markets at once.

Director Felix Umarov built a "stylised comedy" with slightly exaggerated acting — so it would work without translation in any country. Shooting took place in Russia: 2 months of prep, 4 shooting days, 4 sets, 16 synchronised swimmers, and a month of post-production.
